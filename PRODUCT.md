# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Israeli shoppers buying on AliExpress, reading Hebrew (RTL). They arrive two ways in roughly equal measure: from Google, searching for a working AliExpress coupon code or for why a code failed at checkout; and from TikTok / Telegram, already aware of Boten and coming to check it out. Mostly on phones. Their job: pay less for a specific product without wasting time on dead codes.

## Product Purpose
Boten (בוטן) is a free Telegram bot, @Boten_IL_bot: paste an AliExpress product link, get back the price in shekels, the discount, and the seller's coupon code when one exists; when none exists it offers to look for a cheaper alternative. The site has two equal goals: send visitors into the bot, and earn organic Google traffic with honest, checked coupon information and guides.

## Positioning
Codes are checked at a real checkout and dated; failures are logged too, with the actual AliExpress error messages. Most Israeli coupon sites copy code lists without checking them. Boten pulls codes per product straight from AliExpress instead of publishing a public list.

## Operating Context
- Static site generated by `build.py` from `data/*.csv`, published to GitHub Pages on every push and daily (expired codes drop off on their own).
- Pages: home, guide "coupon types", guide "code not working", about, 404.
- Deals channel on Telegram: https://t.me/Boten_deals. TikTok page promotes the bot.
- Affiliate model: AliExpress affiliate links in the bot; the site carries a disclosure.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Coupon codes are not published openly on the site; codes are delivered through the bot (affiliate link). The site may show discount amounts, minimums and validity, with a button into the bot.
- Platform codes (ILAFF…) are only shown when they have an official validity window.
- No tracking cookies. Hebrew, RTL, `lang="he"`.
- Plain static HTML/CSS from a Python generator; no framework, no build toolchain beyond Python.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: בוטן (Boten). Telegram handle @Boten_IL_bot.
- The owner gave full visual freedom, logo included.
- Voice: short, direct, honest; "sell without selling" (owner preference): minimal text, no hard sell.

## Evidence on Hand
- `data/checks.csv`: real checkout checks with dates and outcomes, including failures.
- `data/seller_codes.csv`: verified seller codes (checkout / portal).
- Real AliExpress error messages captured on 18/10/2026.
- `assets/logo.png`: current mascot (peanut robot with a % tag, on red).
- No testimonials, user counts, or savings totals exist. Do not invent them.

## Product Principles
1. Proof over promises: every claim on the page is something we checked, with a date.
2. The bot is the product; the site is the honest front door to it.
3. Respect the shopper's time: answer first, explain second.
4. Never bait: no fake urgency, no fake codes, no invented numbers.
